## Publication checklist
1. Interview an actual instructor or capture a real class/community moment; confirm names and facts.
2. Write a helpful answer to one question (around 400–700 words, when appropriate).
3. Pick an authentic, consent-cleared training photo; provide a descriptive alt text.
4. Add an entry in `src/data/advice.ts` (slug, original title, accurate date, concise summary, image, body sections, and related class).
5. Check mobile layout, links, preview, and correct local class schedule.
6. Review for student privacy, false claims, duplicate text, and local relevance.
7. Publish through the normal site deployment, confirm the article is live, and check the sitemap.
8. Share an excerpt and the new URL on Instagram/Facebook and revisit results through analytics.
