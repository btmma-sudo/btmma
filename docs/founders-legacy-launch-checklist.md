# Founders Legacy Experience — Launch Checklist
Owner: Burning Tree Mixed Martial Arts
Status: staging — do not collect membership payments until registration/compliance and written agreement are verified.

## Offer shown to families
- Initial membership: $600 total for six months, presented as six $100 monthly installments (rather than $600 collected in advance).
- Eligible group classes in the agreed program/age group, subject to schedule and capacity.
- Two private coaching check-ins during the initial six months, by appointment.
- Personalized 24-month progress roadmap, instructor feedback approximately every 90 days, milestone recognition.
- Guest invitations/leadership consideration when eligible; no automatic entitlement to invitation-only leadership or paid events.
- Optional, separately signed 18-month continuation at $169 per month. It does not start automatically.
- $3,642 potential 24-month tuition total; $894 lower than 24 months at $189/month. Excludes applicable fees/optional purchases.
- No belt advancement, 24-month graduation, or results guaranteed.

## Why installments now
Maryland Attorney General guidance: self-defense schools must register; collecting more than three months of payments in advance generally requires posted financial security. Verify Burning Tree's status and get the Attorney General's current notice language before activating any checkout or taking membership payments.

## Parent experience
1. Parent sees the Founders page or uses the existing $29 special.
2. Parent submits a Founders inquiry using the site's existing lead form.
3. Burning Tree contacts parent, discusses schedule and program, provides written terms and required notices.
4. Parent signs separate six-month membership; payment begins only after valid acceptance.
5. Instructor schedules initial goal review and coaching check-ins.
6. Progress feedback near days 90 and 180; recognition near month 6.
7. Parent is offered the optional 18-month agreement with full terms and explicit consent. If declined, initial membership ends and no continuation tuition is charged.
8. If accepted, 18 months at $169/month, with further progress feedback and milestones; memberships do not automatically renew at month 24.

## Operational capacity
- Do not advertise a 15-student limit unless that is the actual cap you can enforce.
- Initial 15 enrollments = $1,500 monthly during the first six months, $9,000 total initial tuition when collected in installments.
- If all 15 elect the optional continuation, $2,535 monthly afterward; this is an illustration, not a retention forecast.
- Prioritize low-cost, scheduled benefits. Avoid unbudgeted gifts and free additional classes that strain capacity.
- Leadership remains invitation-only; belt promotion by skill/testing only.
